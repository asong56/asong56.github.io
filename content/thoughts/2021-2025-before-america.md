When I was in 4th grade, which was in a common chinese elementary school, I was deeply distracted by playing games and reading web novels. I became familiar with “coasting”, which means that I seem to do many things but actually nothing. At 5th grade, which was an important point to get into middle school, I used only one month to study, not very seriously, and I got into one of the best three public schools in Shanghai, through an exam and competing with 2300 other Shanghai students. And I found that I could still succeed if I didn’t work hard. When I was in middle school, my grade was in the middle and I was still stuck in the mud of games and novels, which caused tens of big arguments in my family. At that time, I had lots of good friends, across the whole grade, and I was also the main force in the school soccer team. I chose number 27.
