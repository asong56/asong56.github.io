# Inbound Marketing

*A systemic framework for growth — from buyer psychology to martech infrastructure.*

---

## I. Buyer Personas: Motivations Over Demographics

A buyer persona is a model of an ideal customer built on **Buying Insights**, not just demographic data.

- **Buying Insights as the Core**: Personas are built on priority initiatives, success factors, and perceived barriers — the *why* behind a decision.
- **The Hero's Journey**: Marketing acts as a guide for a prospect on a quest to solve specific challenges.
- **Strategic Exclusion**: **Negative Personas** filter out low-fit leads, concentrating resources on high-value segments.
- **Journey Alignment**: Content must match the buyer's cognitive state — from awareness of a symptom → evaluation of solution categories → vendor selection.

---

## II. Behavioral Analysis: Signal Detection & Segmentation

Behavioral marketing treats user actions as data points for intent and timing.

- **Interaction Tracking**: High-intent signals include CTA engagement, time on technical pages, and visit frequency.
- **Intent Thresholds**: Quantitative triggers for personalized follow-up (e.g., three visits to a landing page without converting).

| Segmentation Type | Source | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Implicit** (Correlation) | Inferred from behavior | Identifying a "dog owner" from browsing patterns |
| **Explicit** (Causation) | Provided directly by the user | Form submissions, account creation |

---

## III. Attribution & Testing

Marketing as iterative science — every outcome backed by a statistical model.

### Attribution Models

| Model | Logic | Best For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **First Touch** | 100% credit to first contact | Demand generation analysis |
| **Time Decay** | More credit as events approach conversion | Short sales cycle analysis |
| **Full-Path** | Credits first touch, lead creation, and final deal | Holistic revenue funnel view |

### The Bullets vs. Cannonballs Methodology

- **Bullets**: Low-risk A/B tests to validate a single hypothesis.
- **Cannonballs**: High-resource investments made only after a bullet provides evidence of success.

### Statistical Significance

- **Variable Isolation**: Test one independent variable at a time.
- **Confidence Threshold**: Results held to 95% confidence to rule out random variance.

---

## IV. Martech & Automation Infrastructure

Growth at scale requires a centralized stack for data integrity.

- **CRM as Data Hub**: Single source of truth — all teams share the same context on a buyer's state.
- **Automation as Force Multiplier**: Beyond email sequences, automation should trigger internal record updates and sales alerts based on behavioral intent thresholds.
