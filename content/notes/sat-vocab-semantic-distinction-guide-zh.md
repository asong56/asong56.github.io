## 1. 沉默与保留　*Silence & Withholding*

**核心张力**：沉默是主动选择还是天性如此？是策略性的还是气质性的？
*Is the silence chosen or innate? Strategic or temperamental?*

| 单词 | 情感方向 | 词义精髓（中文） | English quality | 与最近邻词的区别 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **tacit** | ◼ 中性 | 不言而喻；沉默或行动本身传达的意思 | understood without being said; implied by silence or inaction | 与 reticent 不同——tacit 描述被传达的事物本身，不描述人的性格 |
| **taciturn** | ❌/◼ 负面偏中 | 天性话少；习惯性沉默 | habitually silent by temperament; speaks very little as a rule | 与 reticent 不同——taciturn 是固定气质，这个人平时就不爱说话；reticent 是有所保留 |
| **reticent** | ◼/❌ 中性偏负 | 对某事有所保留；刻意不透露信息或感受 | reluctant to speak about a particular topic; deliberately withholding information or feelings | 与 taciturn 不同——reticent 是对某件具体事情保持缄默，不是整体话少 |
| **reserved** | ◼ 中性 | 不轻易表露情感或意见；保持情感距离 | slow to reveal feelings or opinions; keeping emotional distance | 比 reticent 更广泛——是整体的情感克制，不局限于某个话题 |
| **laconic** | ◼/❌ 中性偏负 | 言简意赅；简短到近乎冷淡 | using very few words; brief to the point of seeming rude or dismissive | 与 taciturn 不同——laconic 不是不说话，是说话极度简短；有时带一种冷峻的力量感 |
| **circumspect** | ◼/✅ 中性偏正 | 谨慎行事；对自己说的话和做的事十分小心 | wary and unwilling to take risks; careful about what one says or does | 和这组其他词不同——circumspect 的沉默源于对后果的考量，是审慎，不是天性沉默 |

> ⚠️ **SAT 陷阱**：SAT 常用 reticent 来迷惑选 reluctant 的人。reticent 专指对言语/信息的保留，不泛指不愿意做某事。考试里 reticent 几乎总是关于说话的。
> *SAT frequently uses *reticent* where students expect *reluctant*. Reticent is specifically about speech/disclosure, not about doing things in general. 'Reticent to act' is technically a misuse but appears in some modern texts — on the SAT, treat reticent as speech-specific.*

<details>
<summary>常见搭配 Collocations</summary>

**tacit**: *tacit agreement* · *tacit approval* · *tacit understanding* · *tacit acceptance*
**taciturn**: *taciturn man* · *taciturn by nature* · *grew taciturn* · *taciturn disposition*
**reticent**: *reticent about her past* · *reticent on the subject* · *remained reticent* · *reticent witness*
**reserved**: *reserved manner* · *quiet and reserved* · *reserved personality* · *reserved in public*
**laconic**: *laconic reply* · *laconic style* · *laconic answer* · *characteristically laconic*
**circumspect**: *circumspect approach* · *remain circumspect* · *circumspect in her statements* · *politically circumspect*

</details>

## 2. 话多与冗长　*Verbosity & Talkativeness*

**核心张力**：话多是能力（表达流畅）还是缺陷（冗余累赘）？
*Is the talkativeness a skill (fluency) or a flaw (excess)?*

| 单词 | 情感方向 | 词义精髓（中文） | English quality | 与最近邻词的区别 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **garrulous** | ❌ 负面 | 话痨式的唠叨，尤指说无关紧要的事 | excessively talkative, especially about trivial things; chatty in an annoying way | 与 loquacious 不同——garrulous 永远是贬义，带有烦人的意味；loquacious 有时可以是中性甚至正面的 |
| **loquacious** | ◼/❌ 中性偏负 | 健谈的；话多但通常流利自如 | tending to talk a great deal; fluently and easily conversational | 比 garrulous 温和——loquacious 不一定暗示说的内容无聊，只是话多 |
| **voluble** | ◼/❌ 中性偏负 | 滔滔不绝；话语连续快速涌出 | speaking or spoken incessantly and fluently; a torrent of words | 比 loquacious 更强调速度和流量——voluble 是话语如水奔涌，不只是话多 |
| **verbose** | ❌ 负面 | 用词冗余；废话连篇到妨碍理解 | using more words than necessary; wordy in a way that obscures meaning | 与 garrulous 不同——verbose 更多描述书面语或正式讲话，强调用词多余，不强调说话态度烦人 |
| **prolix** | ❌ 负面 | 冗长到令人厌倦；篇幅极度拖沓 | tediously lengthy; using far more words than needed, to the point of boring the reader | 这组词里负面程度最强——prolix 不只是废话多，而是拖沓到让人精疲力竭 |
| **diffuse** | ❌ 负面 | 散漫无焦点；（文章或讲话）漫无目的、缺乏中心 | spread out or lacking focus; (of writing/speech) rambling and hard to follow | 与 verbose 不同——diffuse 的问题是缺乏焦点和结构，不只是字数多 |

> ⚠️ **SAT 陷阱**：容易混淆 verbose 和 voluble。verbose 几乎专指书面语；voluble 是口头的。另外 garrulous 永远是贬义，loquacious 不一定是。
> *Students confuse *verbose* with *voluble*. Verbose is almost always about written language; voluble is about spoken delivery. Also: *garrulous* is always negative; *loquacious* is not always.*

<details>
<summary>常见搭配 Collocations</summary>

**garrulous**: *garrulous old man* · *garrulous neighbor* · *garrulous by nature* · *garrulous and tiresome*
**loquacious**: *loquacious host* · *unusually loquacious* · *loquacious in interviews* · *loquacious charm*
**voluble**: *voluble speaker* · *voluble in her defense* · *voluble explanations* · *voluble and animated*
**verbose**: *verbose writing* · *verbose explanation* · *verbose style* · *unnecessarily verbose*
**prolix**: *prolix prose* · *prolix speaker* · *prolix and meandering* · *criticize as prolix*
**diffuse**: *diffuse argument* · *diffuse writing style* · *diffuse and unfocused* · *too diffuse to be useful*

</details>

## 3. 批评与谴责　*Criticism & Condemnation*

**核心张力**：批评是正式的还是私人的？温和还是严厉？针对行为还是品格？
*Is the criticism formal or personal? Mild or severe? About behavior or character?*

| 单词 | 情感方向 | 词义精髓（中文） | English quality | 与最近邻词的区别 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **censure** | ❌ 负面 | （尤指正式或官方地）严厉谴责 | express severe disapproval, especially formally or officially | 比 criticize 更正式——censure 带有官方或公开谴责的性质，有制度性的力量 |
| **reprimand** | ❌ 负面 | （尤指上级）正式训斥 | rebuke formally, especially by someone in authority | 与 censure 不同——reprimand 通常是私下的、有层级关系的训斥，如上司训下属 |
| **rebuke** | ❌ 负面 | 严厉斥责；当面表达强烈不满 | express sharp disapproval or criticism; a stern reprimand | 比 reprimand 更有情绪性——rebuke 带有当下的愤怒或道德指责，不只是程序性的训诫 |
| **admonish** | ◼/❌ 中性偏负 | 温和但郑重地告诫；劝人改正 | warn or reprimand gently but seriously; urge someone to correct their behavior | 这组词里语气最轻——admonish 是关切式的告诫，更像在提醒纠正，而不是惩罚或表达愤怒 |
| **castigate** | ❌ 负面 | 严厉且持续地痛批 | reprimand or criticize severely and at length | 与 rebuke 不同——castigate 是持续性的猛烈批评，不是一句话的斥责 |
| **vituperate** | ❌ 负面 | 用激烈甚至辱骂性的语言痛骂 | blame or insult in strong or violent language; berate abusively | 这组词里最极端——vituperate 已接近言语攻击，不只是批评，而是用侮辱性语言猛烈痛骂 |
| **lambaste** | ❌ 负面 | 猛烈抨击；大力批评 | criticize harshly; attack verbally in an energetic and sustained way | 强度与 castigate 相近，但语域更口语化——在新闻报道和评论里更常见 |

> ⚠️ **SAT 陷阱**：admonish 是最常见的陷阱——看起来严重其实最温和。另外 censure 需要正式或制度性的语境，不能随便用在日常批评上。
> **Admonish* is the most common trap: it looks severe but is actually the mildest here. Also watch the direction of *censure* — it requires an institution or formal context; you cannot casually censure a friend.*

<details>
<summary>常见搭配 Collocations</summary>

**censure**: *censure a politician* · *vote to censure* · *public censure* · *formal censure*
**reprimand**: *reprimand an employee* · *officially reprimanded* · *receive a reprimand* · *sharp reprimand*
**rebuke**: *rebuke sharply* · *public rebuke* · *rebuke for carelessness* · *rebuke from the judge*
**admonish**: *admonish to be careful* · *admonished by the judge* · *admonish gently* · *admonish against recklessness*
**castigate**: *castigate the government* · *castigate harshly* · *publicly castigated* · *castigate for incompetence*
**vituperate**: *vituperate against rivals* · *vituperative attack* · *vituperate endlessly* · *vituperate in print*
**lambaste**: *lambaste the policy* · *lambasted in the press* · *lambaste publicly* · *lambaste for failures*

</details>

## 4. 赞美与奉承　*Praise & Flattery*

**核心张力**：赞美是真诚的还是有目的的？是对等的还是过度的？
*Is the praise genuine or strategic? Deserved or excessive?*

| 单词 | 情感方向 | 词义精髓（中文） | English quality | 与最近邻词的区别 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **laud** | ✅ 正面 | 高度赞扬，尤指公开或正式场合 | praise highly, especially in a public or formal context | 真诚的赞扬，没有过度或有目的的含义 |
| **extol** | ✅ 正面 | 热情洋溢地大力称颂 | praise enthusiastically and at length | 比 laud 更热烈持续——extol 是反复用力地称颂，不只是说一句好话 |
| **eulogize** | ✅ 正面 | 以演讲或文字热情颂扬；发表颂词 | praise highly in speech or writing; deliver a eulogy for | 与 laud 不同——eulogize 常带有正式的、甚至悼念性的语境，多用于颂词或纪念文章 |
| **lionize** | ↔ 混合 | 将某人捧成名人；过度追捧 | treat as a celebrity or object of great public interest | 与 laud 不同——lionize 不只是言语上的赞美，而是社会上的追捧；可能是夸大其词的 |
| **fawn** | ❌ 负面 | 过分奉承；谄媚讨好 | give excessively flattering attention; be obsequiously complimentary | 这组词里唯一明确负面的——fawn 的赞美是不真诚的、出于私利的讨好 |
| **adulate** | ❌ 负面 | 过度崇拜；盲目赞扬 | praise or admire excessively or obsequiously | 与 fawn 不同——adulate 可以是真诚的，但是不加辨别地过度崇拜，如粉丝对偶像的盲目崇拜 |

> ⚠️ **SAT 陷阱**：lionize 看起来正面，但在 SAT 语境里常带轻微讽刺——暗示关注程度可能超过了应得的。adulate 和 fawn 都负面，但原因不同：adulate 是盲目过度，fawn 是虚情假意。
> **Lionize* looks positive but often carries mild irony on the SAT — the implication is that the attention may exceed what's deserved. *Adulate* and *fawn* are both negative but for different reasons: adulate = blind excess, fawn = strategic insincerity.*

<details>
<summary>常见搭配 Collocations</summary>

**laud**: *laud the achievement* · *widely lauded* · *laudatory review* · *laud as groundbreaking*
**extol**: *extol the virtues of* · *extol someone's courage* · *extol endlessly* · *extol as a model*
**eulogize**: *eulogize the deceased* · *eulogize in print* · *eulogized as a hero* · *eulogize at length*
**lionize**: *lionized by the press* · *lionize a writer* · *lionized in literary circles* · *unfairly lionized*
**fawn**: *fawn over celebrities* · *fawn on the boss* · *fawn and flatter* · *sickeningly fawning*
**adulate**: *adulate a leader* · *adulated by fans* · *uncritical adulation* · *adulate without reservation*

</details>

## 5. 欺骗与掩饰　*Deception & Concealment*

**核心张力**：欺骗是主动的（撒谎）还是被动的（隐瞒）？是蓄意的还是冲动的？
*Is the deception active (lying) or passive (concealing)? Premeditated or impulsive?*

| 单词 | 情感方向 | 词义精髓（中文） | English quality | 与最近邻词的区别 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **dissemble** | ❌ 负面 | 掩饰真实动机或感受；假装 | conceal one's true motives or feelings under a false appearance | 与 deceive 不同——dissemble 是隐藏真相，而不是主动说谎；是掩饰而非欺骗 |
| **dissimulate** | ❌ 负面 | 隐藏真实感受或信仰 | conceal or disguise one's true feelings or beliefs | 与 dissemble 几乎同义，但更正式，更多指内心状态（情感、信仰）的掩饰，而不是行为上的伪装 |
| **prevaricate** | ❌ 负面 | 回避；说话含糊其辞，不正面回答 | speak or act evasively; avoid giving a direct answer | 与 dissemble 不同——prevaricate 是说话上的回避，绕来绕去不正面回答，不一定撒谎但刻意含糊 |
| **equivocate** | ❌ 负面 | 故意用模棱两可的语言掩盖真相或逃避立场 | use ambiguous language to conceal the truth or avoid commitment | 与 prevaricate 不同——equivocate 是通过歧义来逃避，说出来的话可以有两种解读 |
| **mendacious** | ❌ 负面 | 不诚实的；习惯性撒谎的 | not telling the truth; lying habitually | 这组词里最直接——mendacious 就是撒谎，没有技巧或策略的含义 |
| **duplicitous** | ❌ 负面 | 两面派的；言行不一 | deceitful in speech or conduct; saying one thing while doing another | 与 mendacious 不同——duplicitous 强调两面性，表面一套、背后一套 |
| **subterfuge** | ❌ 负面 | 为逃避责任或掩盖真相而使用的手段或借口 | a trick or excuse used to avoid an obligation or reveal the truth | 这组词里唯一的名词——subterfuge 指具体的欺骗手段，不描述人 |

> ⚠️ **SAT 陷阱**：prevaricate 和 equivocate 最容易混淆。两者都是回避，但方式不同：prevaricate 是绕开问题，equivocate 是用模糊语言。两者都不等于直接撒谎。
> **Prevaricate* and *equivocate* are the most commonly confused. Both involve evasion but through different means: prevaricate = dodging questions, equivocate = using words with double meanings. Neither technically means lying.*

<details>
<summary>常见搭配 Collocations</summary>

**dissemble**: *dissemble one's intentions* · *skilled at dissembling* · *dissemble emotion* · *unable to dissemble*
**dissimulate**: *dissimulate anxiety* · *dissimulate one's views* · *practiced at dissimulating* · *forced to dissimulate*
**prevaricate**: *prevaricate under questioning* · *stop prevaricating* · *prevaricate endlessly* · *prevaricate about the details*
**equivocate**: *equivocate on the issue* · *stop equivocating* · *equivocate deliberately* · *equivocate to avoid blame*
**mendacious**: *mendacious politician* · *mendacious account* · *notoriously mendacious* · *mendacious claim*
**duplicitous**: *duplicitous behavior* · *duplicitous ally* · *duplicitous by nature* · *duplicitous tactics*
**subterfuge**: *resort to subterfuge* · *achieve by subterfuge* · *elaborate subterfuge* · *without subterfuge*

</details>

## 6. 固执与抵抗　*Stubbornness & Resistance*

**核心张力**：抵抗是有原则的还是单纯的固执？令人钦佩还是令人沮丧？
*Is the resistance principled or merely pigheaded? Admirable or frustrating?*

| 单词 | 情感方向 | 词义精髓（中文） | English quality | 与最近邻词的区别 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **obstinate** | ❌ 负面 | 固执地拒绝改变意见或行为 | stubbornly refusing to change opinion or behavior despite argument or persuasion | 基本的固执词，偏负面——只描述顽固，不暗示原因 |
| **obdurate** | ❌ 负面 | （尤指面对情感诉求时）铁石心肠地拒绝改变 | stubbornly refusing to change, especially in the face of emotional appeals | 比 obstinate 更强调情感硬化——obdurate 是面对同情或恳求也不为所动 |
| **intransigent** | ❌ 负面 | 不妥协的；尤指在政治或谈判中坚不让步 | refusing to agree or compromise; uncompromising, especially in political contexts | 与 obstinate 不同——intransigent 更多用于正式的政治或谈判语境 |
| **refractory** | ❌ 负面 | 难以管控的；不服管教的 | stubbornly disobedient or unmanageable; resistant to treatment or control | 与其他词不同——refractory 也可以用于技术语境（材料、疾病），指对处理或治疗有抵抗性 |
| **recalcitrant** | ❌ 负面 | 不服从权威的；顽固不配合的 | having an obstinately uncooperative attitude toward authority or discipline | 与 refractory 相近，但更强调对权威和规则的抵制 |
| **tenacious** | ✅ 正面 | 坚持不懈的；顽强执着的 | holding firmly to something; persistent and determined | 这组词里唯一正面的——tenacious 把固执包装成了对目标的顽强追求，是褒义的坚持 |
| **dogged** | ✅/◼ 正面偏中 | 坚韧不拔的；执着而不屈的 | having or showing tenacity and grim persistence | 与 tenacious 相近，但更强调吃力的、不那么光彩的坚持——dogged 暗示这很费力，不是轻松自然的 |

> ⚠️ **SAT 陷阱**：注意文章语气——同样是不改变主意，作者欣赏时用 tenacious，批评时用 obstinate。不要只看行为描述，要看作者态度。
> *Students often pick *tenacious* when the context is clearly negative, or *obstinate* when the context is clearly admiring. Check the passage's tone — the same behavior (not changing one's mind) is tenacious when the author approves and obstinate when they don't.*

<details>
<summary>常见搭配 Collocations</summary>

**obstinate**: *obstinate refusal* · *obstinate in her views* · *remain obstinate* · *obstinate resistance*
**obdurate**: *obdurate in his refusal* · *remain obdurate* · *obdurate heart* · *seemingly obdurate*
**intransigent**: *intransigent stance* · *politically intransigent* · *intransigent faction* · *remain intransigent*
**refractory**: *refractory patient* · *refractory child* · *refractory material* · *prove refractory*
**recalcitrant**: *recalcitrant student* · *recalcitrant employee* · *prove recalcitrant* · *recalcitrant behavior*
**tenacious**: *tenacious defender* · *tenacious grip* · *tenacious in pursuit* · *tenacious spirit*
**dogged**: *dogged determination* · *dogged pursuit* · *dogged refusal to quit* · *doggedly persistent*

</details>

## 7. 冷漠与疏离　*Indifference & Detachment*

**核心张力**：疏离是情感缺陷还是理智美德（客观性）？
*Is the detachment an emotional failing or an intellectual virtue (objectivity)?*

| 单词 | 情感方向 | 词义精髓（中文） | English quality | 与最近邻词的区别 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **apathetic** | ❌ 负面 | 对任何事都缺乏兴趣或关心 | showing or feeling no interest, enthusiasm, or concern | 最普通的词——apathetic 描述完全缺乏情感投入 |
| **indifferent** | ◼/❌ 中性偏负 | 对某事漠不关心；不偏向任何一方 | having no particular interest or sympathy; neither for nor against | 与 apathetic 不同——indifferent 有时可以是中性的"无所谓"，不一定是负面的冷漠 |
| **dispassionate** | ✅/◼ 正面偏中 | 不受强烈情感影响的；冷静客观的 | not influenced by strong emotion; calm and impartial | 与 apathetic 不同——dispassionate 通常是褒义，暗示有价值的客观公正 |
| **detached** | ◼ 中性 | 不介入、不动情；情感上与事件保持距离 | not involved or concerned; emotionally removed from a situation | 与 dispassionate 相近，但更描述性、少评价——detached 只陈述情感距离，不作价值判断 |
| **phlegmatic** | ◼ 中性 | 迟钝冷静的；不易激动的性情 | having an unemotional and stolidly calm disposition; not easily excited | 与 apathetic 不同——phlegmatic 描述气质，这个人可以参与，只是天性不容易激动 |
| **aloof** | ❌/◼ 负面偏中 | 冷淡的；保持社交或情感距离 | not friendly or forthcoming; keeping one's distance emotionally or socially | 与 detached 不同——aloof 强调社交上的冷淡，可能被他人感受为不友好或高傲 |

> ⚠️ **SAT 陷阱**：dispassionate 最容易被误读——看起来像"没有激情"（负面），但在 SAT 里几乎总是正面的，意味着冷静客观。注意看语境是在表扬还是批评这种情感距离。
> **Dispassionate* is the most commonly misread in this group — it looks like it should mean 'without passion' (negative) but on the SAT it's almost always positive, meaning calm and objective. Check whether the context is praising or criticizing the person's emotional distance.*

## 8. 热情与狂热　*Enthusiasm & Fervor*

**核心张力**：热情是令人钦佩的（充满激情）还是过度的（狂热）？
*Is the enthusiasm admirable (passionate) or excessive (fanatical)?*

| 单词 | 情感方向 | 词义精髓（中文） | English quality | 与最近邻词的区别 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ardent** | ✅ 正面 | 热情洋溢的；充满激情的 | enthusiastic or passionate; burning with a feeling | 正面的热情——ardent 是真诚、持续的激情，不暗示过度 |
| **fervent** | ✅ 正面 | 热切的；强烈而真诚的 | having or displaying a passionate intensity | 与 ardent 相近，但更聚焦于某个具体的感受或事业，不是整体性格 |
| **zealous** | ✅/◼ 正面偏中 | 对某事业充满热情的；积极进取的 | having or showing great energy or enthusiasm in pursuit of a cause | 与 ardent 不同——zealous 强调付诸行动，不只是感受强烈，而是积极努力地追求 |
| **fanatical** | ❌ 负面 | 狂热的；偏执地执着于某事 | filled with excessive and single-minded zeal; unreasoningly enthusiastic | 与 zealous 不同——fanatical 是过度到失去理性和比例感的狂热 |
| **fervid** | ◼/❌ 中性偏负 | 极度热情的；有时是过度的激烈 | intensely enthusiastic or passionate, often excessively so | 与 fervent 不同——fervid 暗示热度接近过度，比 fervent 更容易带负面色彩 |
| **ebullient** | ✅ 正面 | 充满活力的；开朗热情、欢腾洋溢的 | cheerful and full of energy; exuberantly enthusiastic | 与这组其他词不同——ebullient 描述活泼欢腾的能量，不是对事业的热切投入 |

> ⚠️ **SAT 陷阱**：fervid 和 fervent 外形相近，但 fervent 几乎总是正面；fervid 常带有轻微过度的负面含义。当 SAT 需要表达'热情但可能过头了'，会选 fervid。
> **Fervid* and *fervent* look nearly identical but fervent is reliably positive; fervid often has a slightly negative or excessive undertone. When the SAT needs 'passionate but maybe too much so,' it uses fervid.*

## 9. 聪慧与狡猾　*Cunning & Shrewdness*

**核心张力**：这种智慧是技巧性的（聪明）还是操纵性的（狡猾）？
*Is the intelligence being used skillfully (clever) or manipulatively (crafty)?*

| 单词 | 情感方向 | 词义精髓（中文） | English quality | 与最近邻词的区别 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **astute** | ✅ 正面 | 善于准确判断形势的；有洞察力的 | having an ability to accurately assess situations; shrewd | 正面——astute 的智慧用于准确理解和判断，没有操纵性 |
| **sagacious** | ✅ 正面 | 有深刻洞察力和良好判断力的；睿智的 | having or showing keen mental discernment and good judgment; wise | 与 astute 相近但更正式、更高级——sagacious 暗示积累的智慧，不只是反应快 |
| **perspicacious** | ✅ 正面 | 洞察力敏锐的；能迅速看穿事物本质的 | having a ready insight into things; acutely perceptive | 与 sagacious 相近，但更聚焦于感知力——perspicacious 的人能看到别人看不到的东西 |
| **cunning** | ❌ 负面 | 通过欺骗手段实现目标的；狡猾的 | having or showing skill in achieving goals through deception | 与 astute 不同——cunning 的智慧用于欺骗或牺牲他人，是负面的 |
| **wily** | ❌/◼ 负面偏中 | 善用间接手段获取优势的；狡猾的 | skilled at gaining advantage through indirect or crafty methods; sly | 与 cunning 相近但稍带玩味——wily 的狡猾有时带有一点欣赏的意味（如狐狸的精明） |
| **calculating** | ❌ 负面 | 精于算计的；为达目的冷酷周密地谋划 | acting in a scheming and ruthlessly determined way | 与 cunning 不同——calculating 强调冷静的预谋，这个人不带情感地周密计划 |

> ⚠️ **SAT 陷阱**：这组词的核心区分是正面/负面：astute、sagacious、perspicacious 是正面用智；cunning、wily、calculating 是负面的操纵性智慧。SAT 常考你判断作者认为哪种情况正在发生。
> *The key split in this group is positive vs. negative: *astute*, *sagacious*, *perspicacious* = intelligence used well; *cunning*, *wily*, *calculating* = intelligence used manipulatively. The SAT often asks you to distinguish which the author thinks is happening.*

## 10. 简洁与精炼　*Brevity & Concision*

**核心张力**：简洁是优点（清晰）还是缺陷（深度不足）？
*Is the brevity a virtue (clarity) or a failing (insufficient depth)?*

| 单词 | 情感方向 | 词义精髓（中文） | English quality | 与最近邻词的区别 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **concise** | ✅ 正面 | 言简意赅的；用少量词语清楚表达大量信息 | giving a lot of information clearly and in few words | 最明确的正面词——concise 是在不牺牲清晰度和完整性的前提下的简洁 |
| **succinct** | ✅ 正面 | 简洁有力的；表达紧凑 | briefly and clearly expressed; compact in expression | 与 concise 几乎同义，但 succinct 更强调简洁本身的力量感 |
| **terse** | ◼/❌ 中性偏负 | 简短到近乎唐突；言语简洁但可能显得粗鲁 | sparing in use of words; abrupt to the point of seeming rude | 与 concise 不同——terse 的简短可能显得不友好或冷淡，有些应该说的话被省略了 |
| **laconic** | ◼ 中性 | 极为简短的；简洁到近乎神秘 | using very few words; brief even to the point of mystery | 与 terse 相近但不带粗鲁含义——laconic 的简洁可以显得冷静、神秘或睿智 |
| **pithy** | ✅ 正面 | 精辟的；简短但意味深长 | concise and forcefully expressive; dense with meaning | 与 concise 不同——pithy 的简洁是特别有效的，一句精辟的话承载的意义超过它的字数 |
| **elliptical** | ◼/❌ 中性偏负 | 省略的；因省略而晦涩或模糊 | leaving out words; obscure or ambiguous because something has been omitted | 与这组其他词不同——elliptical 专指省略造成的简短，省去的内容使意义更难理解，是可能让人困惑的简洁 |

> ⚠️ **SAT 陷阱**：terse 和 laconic 都是简短，但 terse 带有负面的社交含义（粗鲁、冷淡），laconic 是中性甚至令人欣赏的。pithy 永远是正面的——是最好的简洁。
> **Terse* and *laconic* both mean very brief, but terse has a negative social connotation (rude, dismissive) while laconic is neutral or even admirable. *Pithy* is always positive — it's the best kind of brevity.*

## 11. 怀疑与不信　*Skepticism & Disbelief*

**核心张力**：怀疑是健康的（批判性思维）还是病态的（拒绝一切证据）？
*Is the skepticism healthy (critical thinking) or pathological (refusing all evidence)?*

| 单词 | 情感方向 | 词义精髓（中文） | English quality | 与最近邻词的区别 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **skeptical** | ◼/✅ 中性偏正 | 不轻易相信的；持保留意见的 | not easily convinced; having doubts or reservations | 基本词——skeptical 通常是正面或中性的，指对声明保持适当的谨慎 |
| **incredulous** | ◼ 中性 | 难以置信的；表达不相信 | unwilling or unable to believe something; expressing disbelief | 与 skeptical 不同——incredulous 是对某件具体令人惊讶的事情的当下反应，不是习惯性的怀疑态度 |
| **dubious** | ❌ 负面 | 怀疑的；可疑的；靠不住的 | hesitating or doubting; not to be relied upon; suspiciously questionable | 与 skeptical 不同——dubious 既可以描述怀疑的人，也可以描述值得怀疑的事物本身 |
| **cynical** | ❌ 负面 | 愤世嫉俗的；认为人都是自私自利的 | believing that people are motivated purely by self-interest; distrustful of human sincerity | 与 skeptical 不同——cynical 是对人性和动机的怀疑，认为人们都是出于私利行事 |
| **sardonic** | ❌ 负面 | 冷嘲热讽的；带有黑色幽默的嘲讽 | grimly mocking or cynical in a way that expresses dark humor | 与 cynical 不同——sardonic 是表达方式，是以黑色幽默呈现的冷嘲热讽 |
| **credulous** | ❌ 负面 | 轻信的；过于容易相信的 | having or showing too great a readiness to believe things | 这组词的对立面——credulous 是缺乏适当怀疑的轻信。放在这里作对照：两端都要认识 |

> ⚠️ **SAT 陷阱**：incredulous 和 skeptical 经常被混淆。skeptical = 习惯性的谨慎态度。incredulous = 对某件具体事情的惊讶反应。一个 skeptical 的人不一定会表现出 incredulous 的表情；incredulous 的人不一定整体上很 skeptical。
> **Incredulous* and *skeptical* are constantly confused. Skeptical = habitual attitude of caution. Incredulous = surprised reaction to one specific thing. A skeptical person might never look incredulous; an incredulous person isn't necessarily generally skeptical.*

## 12. 慷慨与挥霍　*Generosity & Extravagance*

**核心张力**：给予是美德（慷慨）还是过度（浪费）？
*Is the giving virtuous (generous) or excessive (wasteful)?*

| 单词 | 情感方向 | 词义精髓（中文） | English quality | 与最近邻词的区别 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **munificent** | ✅ 正面 | 慷慨大方的；出手远超通常水准 | more generous than is usual or necessary; lavishly generous | 最明确正面——munificent 是大规模的慷慨，不暗示浪费 |
| **magnanimous** | ✅ 正面 | 宽宏大量的；尤指对竞争对手或敌人的大度 | generous or forgiving, especially toward rivals or enemies; noble in spirit | 与 munificent 不同——magnanimous 是情感或道德上的慷慨，比如宽恕，不是物质上的给予 |
| **lavish** | ↔ 混合 | 奢华的；给予或使用远超所需 | sumptuously rich or elaborate; giving or using more than necessary | 与 munificent 不同——lavish 是中性偏正面，但常暗示规模炫耀或奢靡 |

| **profligate** | ❌ 负面 | 放荡挥霍的；在资源使用上极度挥霍 | recklessly extravagant or wasteful in the use of resources; licentious | 比 prodigal 更强——profligate 暗示既有财务上的挥霍，也有道德上的放纵 |
| **penurious** | ❌ 负面 | 吝啬的；极不愿意花钱 | excessively unwilling to spend money; miserly | 对立面——penurious 是吝啬。放在这里与 prodigal 和 profligate 对照 |

## 13. 短暂与持久　*Transience & Permanence*

**核心张力**：短暂是缺陷（转瞬即逝）还是使事物珍贵的特质（昙花一现）？
*Is the short duration a flaw (fleeting) or a quality that makes something precious (ephemeral)?*

| 单词 | 情感方向 | 词义精髓（中文） | English quality | 与最近邻词的区别 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ephemeral** | ◼/✅ 中性偏正 | 短暂的；昙花一现的，常因此而珍贵 | lasting for a very short time; fleeting but often beautiful or precious because of it | 可以是正面或带有惋惜的——ephemeral 常暗示某物正因短暂而珍贵 |
| **transient** | ◼ 中性 | 短暂的；过客式的 | lasting only for a short time; passing through | 比 ephemeral 更中性——transient 只是陈述无常，没有情感色彩 |
| **evanescent** | ◼/✅ 中性偏正 | 转瞬消逝的；很快消散的 | soon passing out of sight, memory, or existence; quickly fading | 与 ephemeral 相近，但更偏视觉或感官——evanescent 是字面上从视线中消散，如雾或梦境 |
| **fleeting** | ◼ 中性 | 一闪而过的；转瞬即逝的 | lasting for a very short time; quickly passing | 最常见、最中性的词——fleeting 用于短暂的瞬间、印象或感受，没有特殊含义 |
| **perennial** | ✅/◼ 正面偏中 | 持久的；多年生的；年复一年的 | lasting for a long or infinite time; enduring; recurring year after year | 这组词的对立面——perennial 是持久的。perennial problem 是反复出现的；perennial favorite 是经久不衰的 |
| **indelible** | ✅/◼ 正面偏中 | 无法消除的；留下永久印记的 | making marks that cannot be removed; leaving a permanent impression | 与 perennial 不同——indelible 是印象的永久性，是无法从记忆或记录中抹去的东西 |

> ⚠️ **SAT 陷阱**：ephemeral 看起来是负面的，但常常不是——SAT 常在欣赏短暂之美的段落里使用它。transient 和 ephemeral 接近同义，但 ephemeral 情感色彩更浓。当文章语气诗意或惋惜时，ephemeral 通常是对的选择。
> **Ephemeral* looks negative but often isn't — the SAT uses it in passages that appreciate the beauty of brief things. Also, *transient* and *ephemeral* are near-synonyms but ephemeral carries more emotional weight. When the passage is poetic or wistful, ephemeral is usually the right choice.*

## 14. 混乱与复杂　*Confusion & Complexity*

**核心张力**：混乱是主题本身的特性（真正复杂）还是表达失败（不必要的晦涩）？
*Is the confusion inherent in the subject (genuinely complex) or a failure of clarity (needlessly obscure)?*

| 单词 | 情感方向 | 词义精髓（中文） | English quality | 与最近邻词的区别 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **abstruse** | ❌/◼ 负面偏中 | 深奥难懂的；晦涩的 | difficult to understand; obscure in meaning | 可以是中性（主题本身困难）或负面（不必要的晦涩）。SAT 里偏负面 |
| **recondite** | ◼ 中性 | 鲜为人知的；晦涩生僻的 | not known by many people; obscure and abstruse | 与 abstruse 不同——recondite 专指鲜为人知，是因知道的人少而晦涩，不是因为本质上难以理解 |
| **arcane** | ◼ 中性 | 神秘的；只有少数人知道的秘密知识 | understood by few; mysterious or secret | 与 recondite 相近，但更带神秘或秘密的色彩——arcane 知识像是被刻意保存在少数人中间的 |
| **convoluted** | ❌ 负面 | 错综复杂的；迂回曲折、难以理清 | extremely complex and difficult to follow | 与 abstruse 不同——convoluted 几乎总是指结构上的复杂，论证、情节或过程弯弯绕绕难以跟踪 |
| **labyrinthine** | ❌ 负面 | 迷宫般的；错综难以找到出路的 | like a labyrinth; irregular and hard to find one's way through | 与 convoluted 相近，但更形象——labyrinthine 的复杂像物理上无法找到出路的迷宫 |
| **cryptic** | ◼/❌ 中性偏负 | 神秘难解的；故意令人费解的 | having a meaning that is mysterious or obscure; deliberately puzzling | 与这组其他词不同——cryptic 暗示刻意为之，晦涩可能是故意的，如密码信息或故意模糊的评论 |

> ⚠️ **SAT 陷阱**：recondite 和 arcane 接近同义，但 arcane 更有神秘和秘密的味道（想想 arcane arts 魔法）。convoluted 几乎总是负面的——是对不清晰思维或结构的批评，不只是中性描述复杂。
> **Recondite* and *arcane* are near-synonyms but arcane has a stronger flavor of mystery and secrecy (think 'arcane arts'). *Convoluted* is almost always negative — it's a criticism of unclear thinking or structure, not just a neutral description of complexity.*

## 15. 衰退与消亡　*Decline & Decay*

**核心张力**：衰退是自然的（老化、熵增）还是道德的（腐化、堕落）？
*Is the decline natural (aging, entropy) or moral (corruption, decadence)?*

| 单词 | 情感方向 | 词义精髓（中文） | English quality | 与最近邻词的区别 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **wane** | ◼ 中性 | （力量、兴趣等）逐渐减弱 | decrease in vigor, power, or extent; gradually decline | 最中性的词——wane 描述自然的、渐进的衰退，不作道德评判 |
| **atrophy** | ❌/◼ 负面偏中 | （因不使用而）萎缩、退化 | waste away, especially from disuse or lack of activity | 与 wane 不同——atrophy 暗示衰退是由于不使用或忽视造成的，本来可以避免 |
| **deteriorate** | ❌ 负面 | 逐渐变坏；质量或状态下降 | become progressively worse; decline in quality or condition | 与 wane 不同——deteriorate 专指质量变差，东西在分解、崩溃 |
| **decadent** | ❌ 负面 | 道德堕落的；沉溺于奢靡享乐的 | characterized by moral decay and self-indulgence; luxuriously excessive | 与其他词不同——decadent 专指道德上的堕落，通过放纵自我和抛弃价值观而衰退 |
| **moribund** | ❌ 负面 | 垂死的；处于终末期衰退中 | at the point of death; in terminal decline | 程度最极端——moribund 暗示衰退接近完成。moribund 的行业快要死了 |
| **enervate** | ❌ 负面 | 使精力耗尽；使衰弱 | cause to feel drained of energy or vitality; weaken | 与 atrophy 不同——enervate 暗示外部原因在耗尽活力，是有东西在汲取能量 |

> ⚠️ **SAT 陷阱**：decadent 看起来只是在'衰退'，但专指通过自我放纵而道德堕落。建筑会 deteriorate，肌肉会 atrophy，但人或社会是因为选择享乐而非美德才 decadent。
> **Decadent* looks like it just means 'declining' but it specifically means moral decline through self-indulgence. A building can deteriorate, a muscle can atrophy, but a *person* or *society* decadates when they choose pleasure over virtue.*

<summary>常见搭配 Collocations</summary>

**atrophy**: *muscles atrophy* · *skills atrophy* · *atrophy from neglect* · *moral atrophy*
**deteriorate**: *conditions deteriorate* · *health deteriorates* · *relationship deteriorates* · *rapidly deteriorating*
**decadent**: *decadent lifestyle* · *decadent society* · *morally decadent* · *decadent excess*
**moribund**: *moribund industry* · *moribund institution* · *moribund economy* · *moribund tradition*
**enervate**: *enervating heat* · *enervated by illness* · *enervating routine* · *enervated population*

## 16. 独创与平庸　*Originality & Banality*

**核心张力**：这个想法是真正的新（创新）还是只是不寻常（怪癖）？
*Is the idea genuinely new (innovative) or just unconventional (eccentric)?*

| 单词 | 情感方向 | 词义精髓（中文） | English quality | 与最近邻词的区别 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **novel** | ✅ 正面 | 新颖的；以有趣的方式不同寻常 | new or unusual in an interesting way | 明确正面——novel 的新颖是有趣且有价值的 |
| **innovative** | ✅ 正面 | 创新的；引入新方法或新思路 | featuring new methods or ideas; introducing change | 与 novel 相近，但更强调实践应用——innovative 的想法不只是新，而是有用地新 |
| **idiosyncratic** | ◼ 中性 | 特立独行的；以不寻常方式具有个人特色 | peculiar to an individual; characteristic in an unusual way | 与 novel 不同——idiosyncratic 是中性的，描述某人独特的癖好，不判断其价值 |
| **eccentric** | ◼/❌ 中性偏负 | 古怪的；偏离正常社会行为的 | unconventional and slightly strange; deviating from usual social behavior | 与 idiosyncratic 不同——eccentric 的偏差是别人注意到并可能觉得奇怪的 |
| **hackneyed** | ❌ 负面 | 陈腐的；过度使用到失去意义 | lacking originality; overused to the point of being meaningless | 对立极端——hackneyed 是熟悉到不再传达任何新鲜信息 |
| **banal** | ❌ 负面 | 平庸的；缺乏独创性到显而易见且无聊 | so lacking in originality as to be obvious and boring | 与 hackneyed 相近但更广——banal 不一定是滥用的短语，只是平淡无聊的普通 |
| **trite** | ❌ 负面 | 陈腔滥调的；过度重复而失去新鲜感 | overused and consequently lacking in freshness or effectiveness | 与 hackneyed 非常相近，都是过度使用。trite 略带一种令人尴尬的显而易见感 |

> ⚠️ **SAT 陷阱**：hackneyed、banal、trite 几乎可以互换，但有细微差别：hackneyed 是过度使用的具体表达；banal 是普遍缺乏新意；trite 是明显到令人尴尬的缺乏新意。如果文章批评一个具体的表达方式，hackneyed 通常最准确。
> **Hackneyed*, *banal*, and *trite* are nearly interchangeable but have subtle differences: hackneyed = overused specific expression; banal = generally unoriginal; trite = obvious and embarrassingly unoriginal. On the SAT, if the passage criticizes a specific expression or phrase, hackneyed is usually right.*
